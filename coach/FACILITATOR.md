# Facilitator guide

Give participants START-HERE.md and QUICKSTART.md. Use WORKED-SESSION.md for a no-personal-data demonstration. Each participant uses their own chosen host; the kit does not collect or upload resumes to an organizer service.

Suggested 15-minute activity: 3 minutes to load readable sources; 4 to check the complete alignment map; 5 to answer one question and approve an accurate edit; 3 to save the draft with its evidence companion. This is a workshop schedule, not a performance guarantee.

If the model loses the format, paste: “Return to the Jobs Night contract. Preserve source IDs and accepted edit versions; give me the complete component map and one next question.” If it invents experience, reject that edit, identify the unsupported phrase, and request a conservative alternative. Never tell a participant to accept an unverified claim to improve a match score.

Use synthetic examples on shared screens. Participants choose what personal data to share with their own host. Do not ask them to post resumes publicly. Accessibility fallback: plain pasted text and short fragment answers; no special interface required.

Success for an attendee: one truthful accepted edit, a clear remaining gap or question, and a saved resume/evidence pair. Do not measure success by an ATS score or a promised interview.
