# Your Jobs Night quickstart

1. Open a new conversation in your preferred AI tool. Paste all of START-HERE.md as the first message (or add it to the host's instruction area if available).
2. Provide your resume and the complete job description. Paste text, attach readable files, or provide a URL/path only when the host can actually open it. If access fails, paste the contents. You can remove your name, address, email and phone first.
3. Check the component map. It should cover the whole JD and distinguish demonstrated experience, transferable work, missing evidence and confirmed gaps.
4. Answer the coach's one question in fragments or paragraphs. “Skip” and “I don't know” are valid answers. Describe what you actually did; measurements are optional.
5. Review each proposed edit and its evidence excerpt. Check that the quote supports every changed claim; a correct-looking source label alone is insufficient. Say “Accept E1-v1,” “Revise E1-v1: ...,” or “Skip E1-v1.” Correct any inflated claim before accepting it.
6. Say “Give me my complete resume draft, evidence companion, change log and portable handoff. Omit contact details from the handoff.” Save both the draft and companion.

## First message after the starter

Resume, version 1:
[paste readable resume text]

Job description, version 1:
[paste complete JD text; add the URL as provenance if you have it]

Please compare the entire resume and JD, then ask me one useful question.

## Files and links

A file path on your laptop is not automatically readable in a browser chat. A LinkedIn or Glassdoor URL may require access the host does not have. Download, copy or paste the relevant content yourself if necessary. The coach must tell you when something was not read. Check extracted text from PDFs, columns, scans and images for omissions.

## Continue elsewhere

Copy the portable handoff into the new conversation after START-HERE.md. Reattach any sources the handoff says are missing. Confirm the current edit versions before applying changes. Omitting contact details from a handoff does not delete prior chat history.
