# Jobs Night Resume Alignment Coach

A conversational AI coach that compares your resume with a job posting, highlights matches and gaps, and asks focused questions to help you describe your relevant experience clearly.

## How to use it

1. Open **[START-HERE.md](coach/START-HERE.md)** and copy the entire file. On GitHub, use the **Raw** view to copy the text.
2. Paste it into a new conversation in your AI chat tool.
3. Paste your resume and the full job posting into the same conversation.
4. Review the comparison and answer the coach's questions, one at a time. Short answers are fine; you can also say “skip” or “I don't know.”
5. Accept, revise, or skip the suggested edits.
6. When you're ready, ask: **“Assemble my updated resume.”** Copy the draft into your resume document and review it before sending.

No installation or coding required—just the prompt, your resume, a job posting, and an AI conversation.

## What you get

- **A job-by-job requirement comparison:** where your resume shows a match, where evidence is missing, and which gaps need clarification.
- **Focused coaching questions:** uncover relevant experience that may be missing or unclear in your resume.
- **Suggested wording:** based on your actual experience and answers, with you deciding which edits to keep.
- **An updated resume:** with your accepted edits, plus separate notes showing what changed and what supports each change.
- **A handoff summary:** so you can continue the work in another conversation.

The coach is instructed to use your real experience and ask before adding unsupported claims.
